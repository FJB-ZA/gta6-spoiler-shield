# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 20 November 2026*

Spoiler Shield for GTA VI does not collect, sell or send any of your data. It has no
servers, no analytics and no tracking.

## What it reads
To find spoilers, the extension reads the pages you visit: their text, tab title,
address, tags and image descriptions. This happens entirely inside your browser.
Nothing it reads leaves your device.

## What it saves
These are saved in your browser's own extension storage, on your device only:

- Your settings (on or off, how strict, how hidden posts look, colours).
- Words you've added and sites you've chosen to skip.
- A running count of how many things it has hidden.
- For a few hours: the address of a link it stopped, so the stop page can open it
  if you choose to. This is cleared when you close the browser.

None of this is synced or shared. Removing the extension deletes it.
