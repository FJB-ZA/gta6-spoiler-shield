Worth changing in Firefox

  • Extensions can’t filter address-bar suggestions. In Settings › Search, turn off search suggestions; under Settings › Privacy, turn off Firefox Suggest.
  • On the New Tab page, turn off Recommended stories and trending searches.
  • Private windows: in about:addons › Spoiler Shield, set Run in Private Windows to Allow.
  • Muting words on X and Reddit, and “Not interested” on YouTube, catch things before they reach the page.

Worth changing in Chrome

  • Extensions can’t filter address-bar suggestions. In Settings › Sync and Google services, turn off Autocomplete searches and URLs and Trending searches.
  • On the New Tab page, use Customize Chrome to hide shortcuts and Discover / story cards.
  • Incognito: chrome://extensions › Spoiler Shield › Details › Allow in Incognito.
  • Muting words on X and Reddit, and “Not interested” on YouTube, catch things before they reach the page.
